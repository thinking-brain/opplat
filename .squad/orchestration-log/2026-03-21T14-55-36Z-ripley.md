## Outcome
Confirmed two clients are intentional. The architecture uses:
- `opplat-client` for tenant-facing SPA (ports 3000/5173)
- `opplat-admin` for SuperAdmin portal (ports 3001/5174)

Separate clients enable:
1. Distinct redirect URI scoping per application
2. Session isolation (prevents oidc-client-ts storage key collisions)
3. Future per-client role restrictions or client-specific mappers

Backend correctly validates `audience=opplat-api`, not client ID. Two-client model is not the root cause of the login error.

## Key Decision
Keep both clients in Keycloak realm. Do not consolidate.
